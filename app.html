Seat Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page splits seats by three textbook formulas using the vote counts you type. It does not know any country's, club's or company's real rules (thresholds, districts, tie-breaks), and it gives no verdict on which method is fair or right. Every figure is your own entry.

Your numbers

Votes for parties A, B, C and so on, in order, separated by commas (1 to 8 parties).

The remainder truth

Each party gets the whole seats in its share, then the biggest leftovers take the rest.

The D'Hondt truth

Each seat goes to the highest quotient of votes divided by 1, 2, 3 and so on.

The Sainte-Lague truth

Each seat goes to the highest quotient of votes divided by 1, 3, 5 and so on.

Seat Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
